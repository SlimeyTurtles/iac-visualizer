ESCF

The Emergent Semantic Cognition Framework: one hub-and-spoke architecture run in three modes — perceptual (categories from covariation, Sim 1a), consequence (outcomes split look-alike berries and assemble a weapon category, Sims 2a/2b), and goal (a purpose reshapes perception via g ⊙ x, Sim 3).
Duplex coding: every feature has a present and an absent unit; a feature that fails to register is coded observed-absent.
One rule throughout: cosine match → competition → fixed vigilance ρ snap (running mean) or recruit. Engagement, not the criterion, sets the level of coarseness.
