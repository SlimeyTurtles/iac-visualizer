What this tests

With localist one-hot inputs, the "4" unit shares zero overlap with any other digit — 3 and 5 are as different from 4 as 9 is. A hub for (5, 3) matches the probe (4, 3) only through the shared "3" unit, and every hub (x, 3) matches it equally well.

So recall for pairs containing the excluded digit blends the votes of all equally-similar hubs: the output spreads flat over their sums instead of peaking at the right one. Watch the output card when testing (4, 3) — that flatness is the model saying localist codes can't extrapolate arithmetic. Distributed / positional digit codes are the natural next step.
