Hubel & Wiesel

Photoreceptors fire faster the more of the light bar is over them. A neuron fires at the average rate of everything wired into it, so it is busiest when the bar lines up with all of its inputs.

Controls
  • Move the light: drag the bar. Drag either end, or scroll, to rotate it.
  • Add: drag Photoreceptor or Neuron from the bottom bar onto the canvas.
  • Connect: drag from a cell’s outer ring to a neuron.
  • Remove: click a cell or a connection, then the red × (or Remove, or the Delete key).
